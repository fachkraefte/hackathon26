ROKIO – PITCH VIDEO · FINAL PROMPT (v3, English)

BRIEF
Produce a motion-design video of exactly 150 seconds (16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps) for ROKIO's live pitch at
the Leuphana AI Hackathon. It plays on a lecture-hall projector, embedded between the presenter's slides and a
live interaction with the audience. It must move the jury emotionally AND show the whole product clearly:
set up a project → report an issue → everyone recognises it → findings → solution & roadmap → culture change.
Language of voice-over, pop-ups and subtitles: ENGLISH. The app UI itself is German (it is the real product) –
show it as is and add a short English caption where a UI text carries the story.

WHAT ROKIO IS (context)
A web app for small and medium-sized businesses. Employees say anonymously on their phone where work gets stuck:
they swipe cards ("I know this" / "not familiar") or hold a red button and talk. AI turns each story into
separate problems everyone can understand. Many voices become a shared picture of where time is lost between
teams – and from that, AI and robotics solutions and a roadmap. The people are the heroes; ROKIO is the turning
point, not the hero.

DUMMY PROJECT (fictional – label it)
Metallbau Heidekamp, a metal fabrication business, 22 employees. Teams: management · engineering · office ·
workshop · installation. Characters: Marco (workshop) – has known for years where work gets stuck, never says it.
Sandra (owner) – asks, but only hears silence.

EMOTIONAL ARC
Frustration & silence (cool, desaturated) → relief "I'm not alone" → clarity → hope (warm).
Before and after scenes reuse the same compositions, only the mood changes.

BRAND & DESIGN
- Colours: paper #F3EFE6 · ink #141824 · logo orange #C66E2C. Signal colours ONLY as signals:
  green #2E9E57 = "I know this" · red #D6402F = breaking point · blue #2F6FDB = "all the time" ·
  yellow #F1E64B = "something is happening".
- Typeface: condensed heavy display font for headlines (Anton or Archivo at ~62 % width), Archivo Regular for text.
- Projector-proof: text at least 48 px, visible at least 3 s, high contrast, 5 % safe margin on all sides.
  Crop UI to 150–200 %, never show whole pages small.
- Phone UI always in a plain dark phone frame (rounded, no manufacturer logo).
- Motion only with meaning: cards swipe, lines draw themselves, numbers build up. Soft ease-out curves,
  no frantic zooms, no glow/particle effects, no 3D logos, no generic AI imagery (no brains, robot hands, matrix code).
- People as calm hand-drawn sepia illustrations matching the deck (ink line drawing, warm paper tones, yellow
  accents) – no photorealistic AI faces. If real footage is supplied, it replaces the illustrations marked [REAL].
- Pop-ups: label bottom left, ink on paper, max. 6 words, 2.5–3 s, soft fade.
- Subtitles for the full voice-over: bottom centre, paper bar with ink text, 44 px.
